📦 MATERIALS GUIDE

Elemental Essences

  • • Fire Essence - Lava stages
  • • Water Essence - River stages
  • • Wind Essence - Mountain stages
  • • Earth Crystal - Castle stages

Rare Materials

  • • Lightning Ore - Thunderstorm stages
  • • Ice Crystal - Snow stages
  • • Star Fragment - Secret stages
  • • Dark Matter - Boss drops

Farming Tips

  • • Play on Hard mode for better drops
  • • Complete side missions
  • • Defeat generals for rare materials
  • • Daily missions reset at midnight

💡 FUSION TIPS

Order Matters

Place higher level weapons in slots 1 and 3 for better stat inheritance.

Save Before Fusing

Legendary fusions have 30% success rate. Save your game first!

Material Boost

Adding 5 materials increases success rate to 80%.

Duplicate Weapons

Use duplicate weapons as materials for guaranteed stat boost.
