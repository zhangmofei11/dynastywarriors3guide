Weapon Fusion Guide

The all-new Weapon Fusion system lets you combine two weapons to transfer elemental properties and skills. Learn how to master fusion recipes and create the ultimate loadout.

New in Remastered Complete Edition Exclusive Feature

What is Weapon Fusion?

Weapon Fusion is a new system introduced in Dynasty Warriors 3: Complete Edition Remastered. It allows you to combine a Base Weapon with a Material Weapon to create a fused weapon that inherits elemental properties and skills from both weapons.

How Weapon Fusion Works

1

Select Base Weapon

Choose the weapon you want to enhance. This weapon determines the final weapon type, attack power, and base stats.

2

Choose Material Weapon

Select a second weapon as the material. The material weapon's elemental properties and selected skills will transfer to the base weapon.

3

Fuse & Receive

Confirm the fusion to create your new weapon. The fused weapon will have combined stats, elements, and skills from both materials.

Attribute Inheritance Rules

Attribute Inheritance Behavior
Element Inherits element from Material weapon. If Material has no element, Base element is kept.
Attack Power Calculated as: Base ATK + (Material ATK × 0.5). Rounded down.
Skills Base weapon keeps 1 skill. Material weapon contributes 1 skill randomly.
Speed Always inherits from Base weapon.
Range Always inherits from Base weapon.
Rarity Takes the higher rarity between Base and Material weapons.

Pro Tips

💡

Use High-Rarity Materials

Higher rarity weapons contribute more stats when used as fusion materials.

🎯

Focus on Dual Elements

Fused weapons with two elements are more versatile in different battle scenarios.

⚔️

Keep Base Weapon Skills

Always try to keep your favorite weapon's signature skill when fusing.

🔄

Experiment Freely

You can always re-fuse weapons. Don't be afraid to try different combinations.
