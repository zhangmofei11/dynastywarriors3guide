MUSOU ATTACKS GUIDE

Master the devastating power of Musou attacks

🔥 Musou Attack Types

Standard Musou

Press R1 + Square (PS2) or RB + X (Xbox) when Musou gauge is full. Deals massive damage to all nearby enemies.

  • • Gauge: 100% required
  • • Damage: High area damage
  • • Duration: 3-5 seconds

True Musou

Activate Musou at 0% morale with low health. Creates devastating explosion with 2x damage multiplier.

  • • Gauge: 0% (health critical)
  • • Damage: 2x standard
  • • Risk: Near-death activation

Raging Storm Musou

Special variation unlocked at max level. Extended duration and larger hit radius than normal Musou.

  • • Requires: Level 30+
  • • Damage: 1.5x standard
  • • Duration: 6-8 seconds

💡 Musou Tactics

When to Use

  • • Surrounded by 5+ enemies
  • • Boss enemy HP below 50%
  • • Officer is about to escape
  • • Defending a critical point

When to Save

  • • Health is already critical
  • • Only 1-2 enemies nearby
  • • Boss has second phase
  • • Near escape sequence
