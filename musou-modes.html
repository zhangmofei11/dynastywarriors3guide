Musou Modes Guide

Master all game modes and their objectives.

Game Modes

🏯 Story Mode

Experience the historical battles of the Three Kingdoms era.

  • • Wei, Shu, Wu campaigns
  • • 5 difficulty levels
  • • Unlock conditions for each ending

Best Tips

Complete all three factions to unlock Chaos difficulty.

⚔️ Free Mode

Select any character and stage freely.

  • • All characters available
  • • Practice combos
  • • No unlock requirements

Best Tips

Great for learning new character movesets.

🎯 Challenge Mode

Defeat as many enemies as possible within time limit.

  • • 3-minute time limit
  • • Score ranking system
  • • Character-specific challenges

Best Tips

Use area-of-effect Musou attacks for max kills.

👑 Versus Mode

Battle against another player or CPU.

  • • 1v1 battles
  • • Multiple arena stages
  • • Character draft option

Best Tips

Practice timing blocks to counter charged attacks.

Musou Attacks Guide

⚡ True Musou

Fill the Musou gauge completely and unleash devastating attack.

  • • Massive damage to all nearby enemies
  • • Invincibility during animation
  • • Different animation per character

🔥 Rage Musou

Activate when health is critical for enhanced power.

  • • 1.5x damage boost
  • • Faster movement speed
  • • Unlocks at 50% health
