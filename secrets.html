Secrets & Unlockables

Hidden content and how to unlock everything.

Hidden Characters

🦸 Lu Bu

The strongest warrior in the game.

How to Unlock

Complete all 5 stages in Musou Mode with any faction

  • • Highest base stats
  • • Devastating True Musou
  • • Unique horse: Red Hare

👸 Diao Chan

Beautiful warrior with devastating combos.

How to Unlock

Complete Musou Mode with all three factions

  • • Fast attack speed
  • • Long reach
  • • Excellent crowd control

⚔️ Dong Zhuo

Brutal warlord of the north.

How to Unlock

Defeat Dong Zhuo in Story Mode (any faction)

  • • Slow but powerful
  • • High damage output
  • • Good crowd control

🦁 Yuan Shao

Ambitious warlord with noble blood.

How to Unlock

Complete Wei faction Musou Mode

  • • Balanced stats
  • • Reliable performance
  • • Decent musou range
