COMPLETE WALKTHROUGH

Master every chapter with detailed strategies

Chapter 1: The Three Kingdoms

Battle 1: Battle of Hu Lao Gate

Lu Bu's forces guard the gate. Focus on defeating his officers first. Use Zhao Yun's speed to intercept reinforcements.

Battle 2: Battle of Xiaoyao Ford

Defend the ford against Zhang Fei's assault. Protect the bridges and eliminate enemy morale.

Chapter 2: The Warlords Era

Battle 3: Battle of Guan Yu's Escape

Guide Guan Yu through five passes. Each gate has a powerful guard. Use mounted charge attacks.

Battle 4: Battle of Yang Gate

Final escape battle. Cao Cao's elite guard blocks the path. Time your musou attacks for maximum effect.

Chapter 3: The Battle of Red Cliffs

Battle 5: Battle of Jiangling

Naval battle sequence. Destroy Cao Cao's fleet before they reach the shore. Use fire attack strategy.

Battle 6: Battle ofyi

Decisive naval confrontation. Target Cao Cao's flagship for quick victory or destroy entire fleet for full clear.

Chapter 4: The Fall of the Han

Battle 7: Battle of Xiaoyao Ford (Wei)

Play as Wei forces. Cross the ford before Sun-Liu forces can organize. Speed is essential.

Battle 8: Battle of Guan Yu

Wei forces pursue Guan Yu. Corner him at Mai Castle for the dramatic showdown.

💡 Walkthrough Tips

Level Up First

Reach level 30+ before tackling hard mode. Higher level means more HP and attack power.

Kill Officers

Officers drop weapons and items. Prioritize captain kills for better equipment.

Use Musou Wisely

Save musou for emergency healing or crowd control. Don't waste on weak enemies.
